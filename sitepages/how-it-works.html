Questions, answered

Everything else you might be wondering before you upload.

Is my resume stored anywhere?

No. Your file is read in your browser and sent only to extract text and generate portfolio content — it isn't saved on our servers.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, DOCX, and TXT, up to 10MB. If a file type isn't supported, you'll get a clear error before anything is processed.

Can I switch templates after generating?

Yes — head back to the upload screen, pick a different template, and generate again. Additional templates beyond Classic Pro are coming soon.

What happens if the AI extraction fails?

PortfolioBuilder falls back to a basic client-side parser so you still get a working portfolio — just with less refined content than the AI would produce.

Does it cost anything?

Your first portfolio is free to generate, no sign-up required.
