Get a cleaner result from your resume

The AI does the heavy lifting, but a bit of structure on your end goes a long way.

Label sections plainly — "Experience," "Education," "Projects," "Skills." The parser looks for these to know where one section ends and the next begins.

Numbered or bulleted projects and roles parse most reliably. Avoid cramming two projects into a single paragraph.

Add your LinkedIn and GitHub URLs directly in the resume header — they're pulled straight into your portfolio's contact section.

Short, specific achievement lines ("Reduced load time by 40%") translate into portfolio bullets far better than long narrative paragraphs.
